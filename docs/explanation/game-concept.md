# Game Concept

## Elevator Pitch

The Moscow Metro is the setting for a struggle over stations, people, and resources. The player controls a faction, develops controlled stations, forms squads, and expands its territory.

Metromaly is a turn-based 2D game with automatically resolved battles. Its primary screen is a map of the Moscow Metro, where stations are nodes and railway sections are connections. The player captures and develops stations, manages the faction economy, recruits units, and assembles them into squads. Multiplayer support is planned.
